# School-year family membership (Issue #2)

## Objective and boundary
Add Odoo 19 family membership for each July 1–June 30 school year, backed either by fully paid invoices or a persistent manually edited family flag, selected by a configurable Odoo setting. MEM-1 is committed; MEM-2 invoice-mode integration is implemented and focused-tested but uncommitted. Manual mode and the combined effective state remain pending.

## Authorized behavior and acceptance
- The 2026/27 period is 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30, inclusive. Other years follow the same July–June boundary; no calendar-year reset of the manual flag.
- In invoice mode, only a currently fully paid qualifying invoice grants membership while today is inside its linked July 1–June 30 period and no posted customer credit note reverses that exact invoice. Full payment qualifies for the entire linked period, not from the payment date; this does not assert historical backdating. A reversal/refund that makes the invoice no longer fully paid deactivates current membership; a posted linked credit note also revokes even if the invoice stays `paid`. Partial payment alone never activates it.
- In manual mode, a family manager changes a family-level Boolean flag; its value remains until manually changed, across school years. The setting determines which mode controls displayed/effective membership, not whether the stored flag is erased.
- The designated `afa.family.billing_partner_id` is selected when issuing an invoice. The issued invoice's billing partner is a snapshot; later reassignment must not rewrite it.
- Family identity and people remain the existing `afa.family` / `res.partner` records. Protect manager-only edits and preserve existing student privacy rules.

## Repo evidence and assumptions to resolve during implementation
- `afa_family` depends only on `base` and `contacts`; `afa.family` has a required billing guardian and no invoice integration. MEM-1 added an independent `afa_membership` addon depending on `afa_family`, `account`, and `product`, and mounted it in the isolated Compose stack. No separate Odoo 16 stack was changed.
- MEM-2 explicitly creates one positive-total product invoice from the active family-period link, snapshots the chosen billing partner, and binds invoice/link with a unique indexed reference. A posted customer invoice with current `payment_state == 'paid'` is the only dues entitlement source; unrelated partner invoices and drafts are excluded. A separate posted customer credit note with native `reversed_entry_id` to that exact invoice revokes even if the invoice remains `paid`; unrelated, draft, or canceled credit notes do not.
- Current payment status and the evaluation date, not a historical paid timestamp, determine invoice-mode membership. Determine Odoo 19 reconciliation/refund semantics with focused tests; do not claim historical entitlement or retrospective backdating.
- The setting is assumed global per Odoo database (`res.config.settings` backed by `ir.config_parameter`), default invoice mode; confirm when wiring UI. Decide explicitly whether manual mode has a date range or represents an undated persistent flag; this plan treats it as undated.
- A tracked repository `AGENTS.md` and `openspec/` were not found during preparation, despite earlier context mentioning AGENTS; the authorized minimal SDD bootstrap now exists. The previous Issue #1 ODD document is historical context, not proof for this feature.
