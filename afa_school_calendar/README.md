# School year terms and non-teaching days

Install `afa_school_calendar` after the membership addon. It extends the
existing **AFA Families → Configuration → School Years** records with a
**Terms** page where a family manager lists the teaching terms of that school
year, for example:

- 1r trimestre: 08/09/2026 to 21/12/2026
- 2n trimestre: 08/01/2027 to 01/04/2027
- 3t trimestre: 18/04/2027 to 20/06/2027

Terms must fit inside the school year (July 1 to June 30) and cannot overlap.
Press **Generate Non-Teaching Days** and every day of the school year that is
not covered by a term becomes a company-wide time off
(`resource.calendar.leaves` without a resource or calendar), so the summer
break, Christmas and Easter appear automatically for everyone. Each gap also
creates an `hr.leave.mandatory.day`, which is what makes the **Time Off**
calendar paint those days. Regeneration replaces only the days generated by
the school year; days without a school year are never touched. Re-run the
button after editing terms or year dates.
**Remove Generated Days** deletes just the generated days.

Open **AFA Families → Configuration → School Calendar Days** to see the same
days on a month calendar instead of a list. Generated days carry the school
year for traceability and filter under the **Generated** search filter;
manual days filter under **Manual**. See the next section for the days that
fall inside a term.

## Manual days: elective holidays and public holidays

Days that fall **inside** a term are never generated — the generator only
knows the gaps between terms. These are entered by hand, and there are two
kinds:

- **School elective holidays** (dies de lliure elecció): the five or six days
  the school chooses each year, for example a carnival day or a local
  pedagogical-patronage day.
- **Public holidays** (festius): state, autonomous-community and municipal.
  They follow the calendar year, while a school year runs July to June, so
  each school year normally carries holidays from two calendar years.

Create each one **once**, in **AFA Families → Configuration → School
Calendar Days** → New:

1. Fill in a name (e.g. "Festiu municipal – Festa Major") and pick the
   **Category**: School Elective, State, Autonomous Community, Municipal or
   Other.
2. Set `date_from`/`date_to` for the closed range (one day: same date).
3. Leave **School Year** empty — empty marks the day as manual.

The module automatically mirrors the day into a **Time Off mandatory day**,
so the Time Off calendar paints it without a second entry. Editing the dates
or name syncs the mirror; deleting the day removes it with it (cascading).
Regeneration and **Remove Generated Days** never touch manual records or
their mirrors, because removals only act on records carrying a school year.
Manage school closures through this menu rather than through
Time Off's own Mandatory Days screen: days created there directly are
overlay-only and do not make the day non-working.

The list and calendar views carry **Category** filters (School Elective,
Public Holidays) and a Group By Category, so the five or six elective days
and the state/regional/local holidays stay organized each year.

The button uses the company working schedule timezone to build full-day
time ranges. The generated days apply to every resource of the company, which
is how Odoo Community models public holidays.

Core record rules limit company-wide time off (`resource_id` empty) to users
with `base.group_erp_manager`, so the module adds one record rule granting that
scope to `afa_family.group_family_manager` and nothing else. Record rules of
different groups are OR-combined, so plain internal users keep the core
"modify own resource" behaviour untouched. Term dates are re-checked whenever
the school year dates change, so generated gaps can never leave the year.

**Known limit:** the Meetings calendar (`/odoo/calendar`) renders
`calendar.event` only, so non-teaching days are not appointments and will not
appear there. They are visible in **School Calendar Days** and as overlays in
**Time Off**.
